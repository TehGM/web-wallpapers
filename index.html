How to use these wallpapers

Every wallpaper here is an ordinary web page, so there are three ways to put one on your desktop. They differ mainly in whether audio reactivity works.

  1. 1

    Use it as a link

    No audio reactivity

    Open a wallpaper with View live and set it up however you like in its own settings panel. When you are happy with it, use the wallpaper's Export option to copy a link to that exact scene.

    In Wallpaper Engine, create a new Website wallpaper and paste that link in. This is the quickest route and needs no download, but a website wallpaper gets no audio from Wallpaper Engine, so anything that reacts to music stays still.

  2. 2

    Install from the Steam Workshop

    Audio reactivity works

    Some of these are published as Wallpaper Engine Workshop items. Subscribing gives you the native experience — full audio reactivity, and updates arrive on their own. Where a wallpaper is available there, its card links straight to it.

  3. 3

    Download the ZIP

    Audio reactivity works

    Grab the ZIP from a card and unpack it. Each archive is a complete Wallpaper Engine project, so pointing Wallpaper Engine at the unpacked folder is all that is needed — it appears alongside your own wallpapers.

    This copy lives entirely on your machine: it keeps working with no internet connection, it never re-downloads anything, and audio reactivity works exactly as it does for a Workshop item.

Setting up an installed copy. A wallpaper installed from the Workshop or a ZIP is loaded from disk, so there is no link for it to read settings from. Set the wallpaper up in your browser, use its Export → scene code option to copy the whole configuration as one line of text, then paste that into the installed copy under Import. It will look identical, and remembers the scene from then on.
